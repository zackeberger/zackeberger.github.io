Welcome!

I'm a third-year PhD student at MIT EECS, advised by John Guttag and Collin Stultz and supported by the DoD NDSEG Fellowship.

My work aims to make proactive health management accessible to everyone by developing AI systems that monitor health, anticipate risk, and can be deployed safely and reliably.

Previously, I was a software engineer at Google working on generative AI infrastructure. I completed my BS in computer science at UCLA, where I worked with Amit Sahai and was a research assistant with the Vision Lab.

I'm also a jazz pianist and comedian! Check me out on Moonshine and Vibe Box.

Contact: zberger [at] mit [dot] edu

Current Projects
